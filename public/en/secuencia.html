Chapter 2 · Sequence and SNAFU

The turn, the activation and SNAFU

A turn is a day. The sides take turns activating formations, and every activation starts with the same old question: how well is the logistics working today?

Game-Turn Sequence

The phases of the turn

On the first turn of each scenario there is no Assignment Phase and no replacements, and the scenario sets the first player. 2.0 · 1.0g

a

Reinforcement

  • One player rolls for the weather.
  • Each side rolls for its Air Points, for the whole turn.
  • Each side rolls for its replacements and applies them at once: each rebuilds one step.
  • The reinforcements on the Order of Arrival are placed.
b

Assignment

  • Assign or unassign Arty Points and Independent units. Whatever is unassigned one turn can be assigned the next.
  • It is the only time units can enter or leave Support.
c

Orders optional

Each formation is given one written order: HQ Move (with its route), Prepared Defense, Fatigue Recovery or No Orders.

d

First player

Each side rolls 2d6; the higher roll must go first. Ties are rerolled.

e

Activations

  • Taking turns, each player picks a formation that is not Done and activates it (this is required; you cannot pass).
  • If one side runs out of formations, the other keeps activating its own until it finishes.
  • Optional: draw activation chits from a cup, or use Activation Smoothing.
f

Turn end

All HQs return to their unused side and the turn marker advances.

Reinforcements · Replacements

Reinforcements and replacements

Both arrive in the first phase of the turn. Reinforcements are new units entering the map; replacements are points that rebuild lost steps. 2.1 · 2.2

Reinforcements 2.1

  • Each scenario's Order of Arrival (OOA) says what enters each turn and where.
  • Through an Entry Area: the HQ and trains are placed on the entry hex in this phase. The other units can wait off-map and move on during their formation's activation, even turns later; while they wait off-map they cannot be attacked.
  • At a specific hex or at their HQ: they are placed now or at the start of their formation's activation. To arrive at the HQ, the formation needs a Complete MSR.
  • They can arrive showing either side, and in Support if eligible. Combat Trains always arrive on their regular side, in any Legal hex that completes the MSR.
  • If the enemy holds the entry hex, enter through another hex of the same area, attack from off-map or wait. EZOCs do not prevent the use of the area.
  • Units that left the map in a retreat come back automatically in this phase, at or next to their HQ.

Replacements (Repls) 2.2

  • There are two kinds: AV, for units with an AV on either side, and non-AV, for the rest.
  • Each Repl rebuilds one step on one unit. They are used as soon as they arrive: any left unused are lost.
  • A unit takes at most one per phase and never goes above its printed steps.
  • There are no replacements on the first turn of any scenario.

    Second Activation · Fatigue

    Go again or rest

    After its Initial Activation, the formation can try another one at once. Doing more in a day is tiring: mind the fatigue. 2.5h · 1.8

    Second Activation

    Roll 1d6, +1 if the formation is Fresh. You must equal or beat the HQ's activation roll (the die on its used side).

    HQ activation roll:

    Formations at Fat-4 or that have just done a Fatigue Recovery cannot try. If you pass or fail, the HQ is Done. A Second Activation with a Partial or Full result always rolls for fatigue.

    Fatigue Increase Table

    At the end of the activation, roll 1d6 for the most likely cause that occurred. If the die falls in the range, fatigue rises one level (at most one per activation).
