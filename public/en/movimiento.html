Chapter 4 · Movement

Movement, terrain and ZOCs

With the formation activated and its OBJs placed (lessons 2 and 3), it is time to move. Each unit moves on its own, with its own MA, paying for terrain by its Movement Class. What stops it is terrain, Traffic and, above all, enemy Zones of Control: not every ZOC affects everyone.

Movement Class

The color of the MA

The number at the bottom right of the counter is the MA; its color says how the unit moves, what terrain costs it and which enemy ZOCs affect it. Flipping costs no MPs, but going from Leg to Tac or Truck needs a Safe Path. 1.1a · 4.0 · 4.3d

16

Tactical Tac

Tracks and armored vehicles. Normal ZOCs do not affect it: it only STOPs on entering an AV ZOC. It is the only class that can make Shock Attacks.

12

Truck

Motorized and towed units. Fast on roads and slow off them. They STOP in any enemy ZOC, and also on entering an Engagement Zone.

4

Leg

Foot infantry, in whatever terrain it takes. It STOPs in any enemy ZOC but ignores Engagement Zones. Deployed, it can enter an enemy Screen's hex and push it out.

Terrain for combat

A defender in that terrain has Terrain: it cancels the Hard Red AV in the open +2 and triggers some Combat Table modifiers. Rivers and streams only count if the attacker crosses them to attack, and never in Engagements, Attacks by Fire or Barrages. 5.0a

Blocking Terrain

It cuts the line between two hex centers: range and Engagement Zones do not pass through. It never blocks into an adjacent hex, and if the line runs exactly along a hexside between a blocking and a non-blocking hex, it is not blocked. 4.5c

Key Terrain

Against a Situational Retreat, the defender can stay in the hex: each unit loses a step, but does not fall back. 5.5d

Terrain rules

  • Several terrains. If the hex has more than one, pay for the most expensive (unless you follow a road). Hexsides are added to the hex cost. 4.1a
  • Pay it all before entering. A unit that has not yet moved this phase can always move one hex even without enough MPs, but never for a Shock Attack, into Prohibited terrain or in breach of a ZOC. 4.1 · 4.1f
  • Reciprocal moves. You can only move from A to B if you could also move from B to A. 4.1b
  • Roads. Following a continuous road you pay its cost and ignore the hex and hexside costs. Roads within a hex connect even if no junction is drawn. 4.1c
  • Halved MA. However many reasons there are, it is halved only once, and fractions are kept. 4.0c
Stacking

Two units per hex

The normal limit is two combat units. HQs, trains, Support and markers do not count. The unit with the longest printed range always goes on top (the higher AV breaks ties); the rest in any order you like. 4.2

Over-Stacking

You can go beyond two, but you pay for it: an adverse DRM on the Combat Table when defending and more rolls on the Barrage Table, which turns the hex into a death trap.

And a unit cannot be over-stacked when using a Fire Event, being the Attack or Assist unit, or entering a hex with a Traffic marker. 4.2b · 4.2c

Zones of Control

EZOC to EZOC

No unit can move directly from one EZOC to another that affects it. It can freely leave the EZOC it began its move in. Terrain does not limit ZOCs, and friendly units only negate them for Safe Paths and HQ movement. 4.3c · 4.3d

Safe Path

Every EZOC blocks the Safe Path of Leg and Truck units. For Tac units, only Real AV ZOCs do. Engagement and Screen Zones do not block it. 1.9e · 1.9f

At the end of a retreat

If the ending hex has an EZOC that affects the unit, it loses another step: all count for Leg and Truck; for Tac units, only Real AV ZOCs. Along the way, ZOCs do not matter. 5.5g

To break free of an AV ZOC, AV units have Stopping Engagements.

Engagement Zones

As far as the gun reaches

Real AV units (not Support, not Light AV, not Screening) project an Engagement Zone out to their range. It is bigger than their ZOC, but does far less. 4.5

What it does

  • Truck MA units STOP on entering.
  • HQs cannot enter it or end a retreat in it, and friendly units do not negate it.
  • It shows where an Engagement can be started.

What it does not do

  • It does not affect Leg or Tac units.
  • It does not cut the MSR or Safe Paths.
  • It does not affect retreats, except HQ retreats.

Line of sight

Nothing blocks it into adjacent hexes. Beyond that, it is cut as soon as the line between centers crosses an intervening Blocking Terrain hex. Ignore the end-point hexes, hexsides, units and markers. On the map above, the woods and the village cast a shadow for the Panther. 4.5c

Screening

Recon screens

A Recon unit on its Move-side can be marked as Screen before its Activities Phase starts, even on a Fail SNAFU (but never in a Second Activation). It slows the enemy without risking itself in combat. 4.6

The Screening unit

  • It has no ZOC, only its Screen Zone.
  • It can move, Recon and spot for artillery.
  • It does not attack, Assist, Attack by Fire or start Engagements, and does not Jump HQs.
  • It cannot end stacked with another unit.

The Screen Zone

  • It reaches 3 hexes, regardless of terrain and units.
  • Enemy Move-side units pay +1 MP per Screen Zone hex entered; Deployed-side units are not affected.
  • Unprepared units cannot enter.
  • It does not affect the MSR, Safe Paths, Traffic or Engagement Zones.

If it is touched

  • Tac and Truck units cannot enter its hex. Deployed Leg infantry can, and pushes it back 3 hexes.
  • It cannot be attacked or barraged, only engaged. Each "Both Loss" becomes "no losses, target retreats 3 hexes".
  • It becomes FINISHED on entering an AV ZOC.
Traffic · Voluntary Retreat

Traffic jams and emergency exits

Traffic

Some results leave it behind: in the defender's hex on the Combat Table (only if it retreats or is destroyed) or in the Firer's hex on the Engagement Table. It STOPs every unit in or entering that hex, though it costs no MPs. If the hex is already full, nobody else can enter, even in passing. 4.0d

Optional rule: Unit Traffic makes units that are not deployed and active negate the roads in their hex. 4.0e

Voluntary Retreat

Instead of moving, an active unit can retreat 3 hexes to get out of a spot it could not leave by moving. ZOCs, terrain, Engagement Zones and Safe Paths do not restrict it.

  • It loses a step, flips to its Move-side and is FINISHED.
  • If the ending hex has an EZOC, it loses another step.
  • It needs a Partial SNAFU or better. HQs can do it even on a Fail, without losing steps, but their formation's fatigue rises one level.
