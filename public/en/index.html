    The drill sergeant's tips

    What nobody tells you on day one

    Classic recruit mistakes. Learn them before your first game and you will save more than one battalion.

    01

    Look after your MSR! Combat Trains off the map are -3 to SNAFU, and on a Fail the formation does not activate. Lesson 3

    02

    No OBJ, no attack. Regular and Shock Attacks, Attacks by Fire and Barrages all need an OBJ Zone; only Engagements are fought without one. Lesson 3

    03

    Tanks do not shoot their way into villages. An Engagement takes no ground: to keep the hex you need a Regular or Shock Attack. Lesson 5

    04

    Three is a crowd. Stacking more than 2 units in one hex is legal, but it gives adverse modifiers and extra rolls to the enemy artillery. Lesson 4

    05

    Count your Fire Events. Each unit has two per activation, and spending the last one STOPs it, even with MA left. Lesson 1

    06

    Not every ZOC stops everyone. Tanks ignore normal ZOCs; trucks stop even in an Engagement Zone. Lesson 4

    07

    The Second Activation is optional. Each one with a Partial or Full result rolls for fatigue, a tired formation rolls worse SNAFUs and at Fat-4 it cannot go again. Go again when you need to, not out of habit. Lesson 2

    08

    Keep the rulebook handy. This academy summarises; the complete rules are in the series rulebook (PDF), and each game's rules override it. Lesson 0
